Our Legacy

A Legacy Founded on Trust

અમારો વારસો

Arbuda Jewellers began its journey in 2001, founded by Shri Ramjibhai Aatosh with a simple belief: jewellery is bought on trust, and trust is earned one honest transaction at a time.

From Palanpur, the house grew into one of North Gujarat's most respected names — known for classic designs, fair dealing and a family-like relationship with every customer who has walked through its doors.

Twenty-seven golden years later, that founding promise still guides every piece we craft and every price we quote.
